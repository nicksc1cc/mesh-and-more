Fit, not likeness

A good table isn't eight versions of the same person. It's a range — conversation moves, pace balances, nobody's the odd one out. Research is clear: groups work on complementarity, not similarity.

We build for range. You come home with friends, not just a completed itinerary.

Is this dating?

No. Travel, not matchmaking. We seat you for company at mealtimes and a week you won't spend alone.

What do you do with my answers?

Use them to place you, and to tell you why. We don't sell them. You can ask to see what we hold on you, and delete it.

Why late 30s to 50s?

Because that's the gap. Under 30 has the hostel. Over 70 has the coach. In between, you're often the only one near your age. We set the range so the table reads as peers.

How does the hold work?

Register for free. If you want a priority place, you can put down a refundable £50–£100 hold. Returned in full if we don't run the departure, or if you change your mind before your place is confirmed.

What if my table doesn't fill?

We don't run a trip with a half-made group. If yours doesn't fill, we refund your hold and tell you, instead of squeezing you in with people who don't fit.
