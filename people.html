Why it holds together

On complementarity, not likeness

A good table isn't six versions of the same person. It's a range: someone to hold the slow pace, someone up at first light, someone who'd rather listen, someone who keeps dinner going. The research is clear — what makes a group work is how the people complement each other, not how alike they are.

That's why we build for range. You come home with friends, not just a completed itinerary.
